# Code of Conduct

We want Instructree to be a welcoming, practical project.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, and do not harass or discriminate against participants. Project maintainers may edit or remove abusive contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior harms the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.
